Research Program

Research Vision

My work aims to decode plant defense mechanisms using computational approaches. Plants rely on complex immune signaling networks, yet many resistance genes remain unidentified. I develop machine learning frameworks that combine sequence features, evolutionary signatures, and expression data to predict functional immune genes.

Current Work

• Prediction of NLR resistance genes using deep learning • Transcriptomic network analysis of defense responses • Identification of transcription factor–defense regulatory modules • Functional annotation of hypothetical proteins

Methodological Contributions

I design reproducible bioinformatics workflows integrating RNA-seq analysis, co-expression network analysis, protein structural confidence assessment, and feature-based classification models.

Future Directions

My future research will focus on cross-species immune gene prediction and interpretable AI models for biological discovery, enabling crop improvement and disease-resistant breeding strategies.
