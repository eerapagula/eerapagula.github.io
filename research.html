Research Program

Vision

Develop computational frameworks to understand plant immune systems and predict defense genes using interpretable machine learning and multi‑omics integration.

Current Work

Deep learning prediction of resistance genes, transcriptomic co‑expression networks, and transcription factor regulatory modules in stress response.

Methods

RNA‑seq analysis, WGCNA, protein structural confidence analysis, sequence feature engineering, and classification models.

Future Direction

Cross‑species immune gene prediction and explainable AI models for biological discovery and crop improvement.
