Computational Plant Immunity & AI-Driven Genomics

I am a PhD research scholar working at the interface of plant genomics, systems biology, and machine learning. My research focuses on identifying plant immune and defense-response genes using integrative multi-omics data and deep learning models.

Research Focus

• Deep learning prediction of plant resistance (R) genes • Regulatory network inference of stress response pathways • Functional annotation of uncharacterized plant proteins • Development of reproducible bioinformatics pipelines and web tools
