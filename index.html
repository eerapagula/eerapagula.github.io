About Me

I am a PhD Research Scholar dedicated to advancing computational biology and plant genomics through innovative applications of machine learning and deep learning. My work bridges biological complexity and computational analysis, leveraging data-driven approaches to unlock insights into plant systems.

My research focuses on understanding molecular mechanisms underlying plant defense and agronomic performance. By integrating genome-wide analyses with predictive modeling, I contribute to sustainable agriculture and crop improvement strategies addressing global food security challenges.

Plant Immunity

Investigating molecular mechanisms of plant defense signaling, including PTI and ETI pathways through genome-wide characterization of resistance genes.

Computational Genomics

Employing advanced bioinformatics pipelines for comprehensive gene family analyses, phylogenetics, and regulatory element identification.

AI-Driven Prediction

Developing deep learning architectures for gene function prediction, protein interactions, and stress response pathway analysis.

Crop Improvement

Translating computational discoveries into breeding applications through meta-QTL analysis and genetic marker identification.
