About

I am a PhD researcher at the Centre for DNA Fingerprinting and Diagnostics (CDFD), Hyderabad, working at the intersection of computational biology and machine learning. My research develops and applies deep learning methods to problems in genome-scale sequence analysis, transcription factor biology, and structural bioinformatics.

Recent work spans genome-wide characterization of regulatory gene families, molecular dynamics of disease-relevant proteins, and sequence-based deep learning. I am interested in extending these methods to single-cell genomics, protein language models, and network-based function prediction.

Deep Learning for Sequences

CNN and transformer architectures for genomic and protein sequence modeling, including cis-regulatory element prediction and function classification.

Comparative Genomics

Genome-wide gene family identification, phylogenetic reconstruction, synteny analysis, and evolutionary characterization across plant and animal lineages.

Transcriptomics & Networks

RNA-seq differential expression, co-expression network analysis (WGCNA), and integrative multi-omic analysis of stress and disease states.

Structural Bioinformatics

Molecular dynamics simulations, protein-ligand interaction studies, and structural characterization of disease-associated proteins.
