Research Overview

I work on computational discovery of plant defense genes using integrative genomics and machine learning. My research combines sequence analysis, transcriptomics, and systems biology to identify regulatory networks and resistance mechanisms.

Focus areas include NLR gene prediction, co‑expression network analysis, and functional annotation of unknown proteins to support crop improvement and disease resistance.
