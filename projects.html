Featured Projects

Research initiatives driving innovation in computational biology

Genome-wide Analysis of NLR Genes in Amaranth

Comprehensive identification, phylogenetic analysis, duplication patterns, synteny mapping, and expression profiling of nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat genes in grain amaranth. This project provides insights into plant immune system evolution and functional diversification.

Genomics Phylogenetics Plant Immunity Expression Analysis

NAC, MYB, bHLH, bZIP, AP2ERF and GRAS Transcription Factor Family Analysis

Structural classification, evolutionary expansion patterns, and regulatory network analysis of key transcription factor families in plant species. Investigating their roles in stress responses and developmental processes.

Transcription Factors Evolution Regulatory Networks Stress Biology

Deep Learning for Plant Defense-Response Prediction

Development of neural network-based classification models with multi-layer feature engineering for predicting plant defense-response genes. Utilizing sequence-based and structural features to achieve high prediction accuracy.
